# Key Insights

## Executive Overview
- Total revenue reached **$14.4M**, with **$4M profit** and a **27.8% profit margin**.
- The business sold approximately **1.1M units** across **35 products** and **50 stores**.
- **Toys** generated the highest category revenue at approximately **$5.09M**.
- **Ciudad de Mexico 2** was the highest-revenue store, generating approximately **$0.55M**.
- Revenue fluctuated throughout the period, with a notable peak around **late 2022 / early 2023**.

## Product Performance
- **Lego Bricks** was the top revenue-generating product at approximately **$2.39M**.
- **Toys** generated the highest revenue and profit among the product categories.
- Revenue is concentrated among a relatively small group of top-performing products.
- Lower-performing products provide opportunities for targeted promotions and portfolio optimization.

## Store Performance
- **Ciudad de Mexico 2** was the top-performing store by revenue.
- **Downtown** locations contributed the largest share of revenue among store locations.
- Revenue is distributed across multiple cities, but leading stores contribute a significant portion of total sales.
- Top-performing stores can provide benchmarks for improving lower-performing locations.

## Inventory Analysis
- Total inventory stands at approximately **30K units**, with an inventory value of around **$300K**.
- **Art & Crafts** holds the largest share of inventory at approximately **9K units**.
- Inventory levels vary considerably across product categories and stores.
- Some lower-inventory categories should be monitored closely to reduce potential stockout risks.

## Business Insights
- The strongest performance is concentrated around **Toys, leading products, and top-performing stores**.
- High-performing products and locations should be analyzed for repeatable sales and inventory strategies.
- Inventory should be aligned with product demand to avoid overstocking slower categories while maintaining availability of high-demand products.
- Store-level performance comparisons can help identify opportunities for operational improvement.

## Recommendations
- Expand inventory and promotional focus on high-performing **Toys** products.
- Replicate successful strategies from top-performing stores.
- Monitor low-stock products regularly to reduce stockout risk.
- Review underperforming products for pricing, promotions, or assortment changes.
- Optimize inventory allocation based on product and store-level demand.
